Service details and care standards

We wash clothes, sheets, and towels with fragrance-free, hypoallergenic detergent, a color-catcher sheet, and detergent booster. We dry on medium heat. Please keep delicates and specialty-care items at home.

Empty pockets and pre-treat stubborn stains. Send your preferred detergent or hangers if needed. Leave out anything soiled with pee, poop, or puke.

We wash towels and linens for fitness studios, salons, cafés, and short-term rentals. Café linens must be free of grease and cooking oil. We don’t accept restaurant laundry.

We use professional-grade detergent, nonbleach sanitizer, and oxygen bleach. Tell us if you need different products. Occasional commercial orders are $2 per pound up to 100 pounds per month. Request a quote for larger or ongoing orders.
