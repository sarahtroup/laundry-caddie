How We Care for Your Laundry

Draft for review

Residential laundry care

We wash clothes, sheets, and towels with fragrance-free, hypoallergenic detergent. We add a color-catcher sheet and detergent booster to every load. We use the same wash and dry process for all items and dry on medium heat. Please keep delicates and specialty-care items at home.

Before pickup, empty pockets and pre-treat stubborn stains if needed. Send your preferred detergent if you’d like us to use it, and include hangers for any garments you’d like returned hanging. Please keep out items soiled with any of the “Three P’s” (pee, poop, or puke).

Residential subscriptions

Your subscription includes 50 weeks of weekly service per year. We close for Thanksgiving week and Christmas week. Your subscription is billed monthly at the same rate during those months because the monthly price already accounts for both closures.

You can cancel anytime with at least seven days’ notice before your next billing cycle.

Residential laundry by the pound

We’ll email you to confirm your pickup time. Thursday pickup is available for by-the-pound service. We weigh your laundry after pickup and send a Stripe invoice based on the measured weight.

Commercial laundry

We wash commercial linens in commercial washers and dryers using gentle-on-skin, enzyme-based detergent, sanitizer, and souring and softening agents. We can customize products to your needs. Request a commercial quote to discuss what we can wash, your volume, and your pickup needs.
