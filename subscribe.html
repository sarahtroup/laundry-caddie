How we care for your laundry

  • We wash clothes, sheets and towels with fragrance-free, hypoallergenic detergent.
  • We add a color-catcher sheet and detergent booster to every load.
  • Everything is washed and dried the same way, on medium heat, so please keep delicates and special-care items at home.
  • Pre-treat stubborn stains and empty pockets before pickup. Have a favorite detergent? Send it along.
  • Send hangers if you'd like some garments returned on hangers.
  • Please don't send items affected by the "Three P's": pee, poop or puke.
  • We close two weeks a year, Thanksgiving and Christmas weeks. Your monthly price already accounts for this.
