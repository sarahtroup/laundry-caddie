How we care for your laundry

  • We wash clothes, sheets and towels with fragrance-free, hypoallergenic detergent.
  • We add a color-catcher sheet and detergent booster to every load.
  • Everything is washed and dried the same way, on medium heat, so please keep delicates and special-care items at home.
  • Pre-treat stubborn stains and empty pockets before pickup. Have a favorite detergent? Send it along.
  • Send hangers if you'd like some garments returned on hangers.
  • Please don't send items affected by the "Three P's": pee, poop or puke.
  • We close two weeks a year, Thanksgiving and Christmas weeks. Your monthly price already accounts for this.

What the secure checkout page will ask for

At launch, the button above opens a checkout page hosted by Stripe with Laundry Caddie's logo and colors. This sketch shows its layout. It isn't a working form.

After checkout

Stripe emails a receipt and brings the customer back to a Laundry Caddie thank-you page. Within 48 hours, the team emails their weekly pickup slot and drops off the first bag.

Managing a subscription

A "Manage my subscription" link opens Stripe's customer portal. Subscribers sign in with their email to update their card, switch bag size or cancel at the end of the billing period. Staff can pause billing from the Stripe dashboard.
