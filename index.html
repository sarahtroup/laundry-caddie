FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does it cost?

Pay as You Go is $2.34/lb (DWB Special $2/lb), with free pickup and delivery on orders of 20 lb or more. Subscriptions are $155/month for a Regular Bag (up to 25 lb weekly) or $185/month for a Large Bag (up to 40 lb weekly). Over a year, that averages $1.44/lb and $1.11/lb.

How does payment work?

For Pay as You Go, we weigh each bag before washing and send you a secure payment link at pickup. Subscriptions charge the card on file each month for the upcoming month.

How fast is the turnaround?

About 48 hours on weekdays. We pick up on Mondays, Tuesdays or Wednesdays, based on availability. After you sign up, we'll email you a weekly time slot for your neighborhood or campus.

What items can't you accept?

We don't clean delicates, dry-clean-only, line-dry or other items needing special handling. We can't take heavy or bulky items like bath mats, quilts, rugs and comforters at this time. We also can't accept items soiled with the "Three P's": pee, poop or puke. Sheets and towels are welcome, and you're welcome to send hangers with your laundry.

Where do you pick up?

We currently serve Downtown/San Marco, Miramar/San Jose/Beauclerc, Riverside/Avondale/Ortega and the Southside, plus the UNF, JU, Edward Waters and FSCJ campuses. Community drop-off sites are also available. We're growing neighborhood by neighborhood, so if you're outside these areas, tell us where we should caddie next.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes. There's no contract. Pause or cancel with seven days' notice before your next billing cycle by calling 904-779-3136 or emailing laundrycaddie@arcjacksonville.org. Email us to change your bag size or add a discounted second bag.

Do I need a special bag?

Subscribers get a Regular or Large bag, and we drop off your first one for you to fill. Pay-as-you-go customers can use any bag.
